# PMP scenario review: brief for the reviewer

Thank you for helping. This takes about **3–5 hours** and can be split over several sittings.

## What this is

A learning game for PMP® exam practice. An AI "assistant PM" narrates handling a project situation step by step. Some steps contain planted mistakes. The learner says STOP at a mistake and explains what's wrong, why, and what the PM should do instead.

These are 10 **original** draft scenarios. None of the text is copied from PMBOK®, the PMI exam content outline, the PMI Lexicon or any real or recalled exam question. *Not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by PMI. PMP and PMBOK are registered marks of the Project Management Institute, Inc.*

## What we need from you

1. **Correctness.** For each planted mistake, is it really wrong by PMI's standards? Is the stated correct action the answer PMI would prefer? Is the severity right (critical, major or minor)?
2. **Clean steps.** Every step that isn't marked as a mistake should be acceptable practice. Tell us if any of them is actually wrong, because then the answer key is wrong. Case 5 is meant to have **no** mistakes at all.
3. **The 15 flagged questions.** Every item marked `[REVIEWER CHECK]` (RC1–RC15, plus G1–G3) is a point where our writer wasn't sure.
4. **Realism.** Would a real PM do or say this? Is the "assistant PM" narrator believable?
5. **Labelling (later, optional).** Once testers have played, mark their answers using the label sheet.

For each point, please say how sure you are and, if you can, which source you'd cite.

**Please don't share the answer key with testers.**
